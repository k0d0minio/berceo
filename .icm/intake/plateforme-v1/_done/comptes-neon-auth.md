# Stub: Accounts on Neon Auth, with consent and transactional e-mail

- scope: plateforme-v1
- personas: parent, professionnel, admin
- initiative: Plateforme Berceo V1 / objective: a first usable version on uat.berceo.be before December 2026, for a launch in January 2027
- depends-on: socle-design-system
- sequence: 2 of 15
- complexity: high
- recommended-model: opus

## Problem

The users table has no way to sign in, no consent record and no e-mail. Nothing in the product can exist until a family or a professional can create an account and come back to it.

## Proposed change

Neon Auth as the provider. A family signs up in three minutes with the guide's fields (prénom, nom, adresse e-mail, numéro de téléphone, mot de passe) under the guide's hook sentence; e-mail verification; sign-in, sign-out, "Mot de passe oublié" with the guide's neutral confirmation. A professional signs up with the same short form and lands on her onboarding (stub 4). Each account has one role (parent, professionnel, admin) and is redirected to its space. Acceptance of the CGU and the privacy policy is stored with the version accepted and the timestamp. An admin role exists and the founders' two accounts are seeded by hand. The transactional e-mail foundation on Resend: the sender, the e-mail layout with the logotype, and the first templates (welcome family, verify e-mail, reset). The users migration adds the auth identity, phone and consent columns.

## Acceptance criteria (rough)

- [ ] A family can create an account, verify her e-mail, sign in, sign out and reset her password, with the guide's copy and error messages.
- [ ] A professional account exists in state "en attente de validation" and is told so at sign-in.
- [ ] The consent version and timestamp are stored per user.
- [ ] Each role lands on its own space; an admin route refuses a parent.
- [ ] The welcome e-mail arrives from Berceo's domain.
- [ ] The migration is generated by drizzle-kit, the journal test passes.

## Out of scope (this feature)

- The professional's onboarding steps (stub 4), the family profile (stub 6).
- SMS phone verification, two-factor authentication [D-27].
