# Stub: Every Belgian commune carries its centre, and the platform measures the distance between two communes

- feature-slug: communes-centres

## Problem

The commune list in `src/lib/communes/data.ts` has no coordinates. The radius of a professional's
zone (`profil-zone-rayon`) needs one point per commune and a distance between two communes.

## Proposed change

Add the official centre of each of the 565 communes, from NGI-IGN AdminVector, to the commune
list. Add one pure function that returns every commune within a distance of a set of communes.
No user sees a change.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `src/lib/communes/centres.ts` holds one centre for each of the 565 REFNIS codes of `data.ts`, generated by `scripts/communes/generate.py`.
- [ ] The header of `centres.ts` names NGI-IGN AdminVector, its download URL, the date and the CC BY 4.0 licence.
- [ ] `communesWithin(communes, km)` returns her own communes and every commune whose centre is within `km` of the centre of one of them.
- [ ] A test holds the 565 centres, one per code, and three known distances: Ixelles to Etterbeek about 1.6 km, Ixelles to Anvers about 43.3 km, Ixelles to Liège about 87.5 km.
- [ ] A test holds that no client component imports `centres.ts`.
- [ ] The README section on the commune list names the source and its CC BY 4.0 licence.

## Out of scope

- Any field, column or reader of the radius: `profil-zone-rayon-saisie` and `zone-rayon-lecture`.
- The Bastogne code: `triage/communes-bastogne-refnis`.
- A map, or any coordinate shown on the site.

## Notes

- The decision and the source are in `.icm/intake/onboarding-retour-1/_done/profil-zone-rayon.md`
  § Notes. Read it first.
- The source: AdminVector, layer `municipalcenter`, field `niscode`, a PointZ in WGS 84 (EPSG:4326).
  The shapefile zip is 100 MB. The script downloads it, reads the one layer, and writes nothing
  else. The GeoPackage edition is an option if a reader is simpler.
- The centre is the official municipal centre, not a centre of gravity. Keep five decimals.
- The script fails on a code that is in one list and not in the other (why: a new merger is
  noticed here, never dropped).
- AdminVector carries Bastogne as `82039`. `data.ts` carries `82003`. When
  `triage/communes-bastogne-refnis` has not shipped yet, map `82039` to `82003` in one named
  override with a comment. Remove the override when the triage stub ships.
- The distance is the haversine distance on a sphere of 6371 km. A test may use a tolerance of
  0.5 km.
- `centres.ts` is server-only (`import "server-only"` or a test that holds the imports), so the
  browser bundle does not grow.
- Files: `scripts/communes/generate.py`, `src/lib/communes/centres.ts` (new, generated),
  `src/lib/communes/index.ts` or a new `src/lib/communes/distance.ts`, their tests, `README.md`.
